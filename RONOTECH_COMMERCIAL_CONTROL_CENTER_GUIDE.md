# RonoTech Commercial Control Center — Operating Guide

1. Start the Control Center on the RonoTech owner/administrator computer.
2. Sign in with the owner account.
3. Add each customer school under **Schools & Customers**.
4. Enter the customer's school/site ID, contact details, installation date, plan and license information.
5. Use **Dashboard** to monitor active, expiring and expired licenses.
6. When a customer renews, open **Renewals**, select the school, enter the new expiry and record payment/reference details.
7. Keep support start/end dates and support notes current.
8. Use **Audit Log** for a record of owner-side changes.
9. Create periodic Control Center database backups.
10. Export commercial records when an offline copy is needed.

The Control Center is an operational CRM/license register. It records commercial license data and can issue signed licenses through the owner-only Control Center. The customer school application still performs its own signed-license verification.
